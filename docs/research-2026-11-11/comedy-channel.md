# Punchline Bazaar: why clip Shorts get ~1 view, and what to change

Research date: 11 Nov 2026. Web research only. "Unknown" means I searched and found nothing reliable.

## 1. YouTube's rules on clip Shorts (including Creative Commons)

**Reach: the October 2026 Shorts change (most important finding).** On 1 Oct 2026 YouTube posted [Prioritizing original content on Shorts](https://support.google.com/youtube/thread/470890423/prioritizing-original-content-on-shorts). It will "reduce the reach of content re-uploaded from other creators" ([9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/10/02/youtube-wont-recommend-unoriginal-shorts/)). The target is "channels identified as primarily aggregating or re-uploading other creators' videos," which "will likely see less distribution within the Shorts feed" ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/re-uploaded-shorts-lose-reach-as-youtube-favours-original-clips/)). The judgement is made on the whole channel, not one video at a time. It is not a ban. YouTube gives no threshold, no start date, and nothing in Studio tells you if you are affected ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/re-uploaded-shorts-lose-reach-as-youtube-favours-original-clips/), [OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-shorts-original-content-update/)).
- What counts as added value: "your own original commentary, unique edits, or storytelling." Not enough: "minor technical edits or template-based changes" ([OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-shorts-original-content-update/)). TeamYouTube: "focus on bringing your own voice" ([Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/youtube/youtube-cracks-down-on-clipping-channels-in-shorts-update-prioritizing-original-content-3415700/)).
- Rene Ritchie (Creator Insider, 1 Oct, reported second-hand): a voice-over that only describes what is on screen does not make a clip original. Neither do small technical edits or bulk template changes. To show someone else's video, share it in the Posts tab. If a channel moves toward original work, its reach gets re-evaluated ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/re-uploaded-shorts-lose-reach-as-youtube-favours-original-clips/)).
- **Unknown:** whether clips used with permission or under CC are treated differently. The post does not say ([OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-shorts-original-content-update/)).

**Spam policy (Community Guidelines, so videos can be removed).** "Scraped content" is banned: re-posting "other videos without adding anything of your own." YouTube's example is a channel that re-uploads popular clips with no "transformative editing, reaction, or educational breakdown" ([YouTube spam policy](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2801973)).

**Monetization (YPP).** The reused-content rule does not allow "Clips of moments from your favorite show edited together with little or no narrative." It "applies even if you have permission from the original creator." Allowed: "Reaction videos where you comment on the original video" and "Edited footage from other creators where you add a storyline and commentary" ([YouTube monetization policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392)). On 15 July 2025 "repetitious" content was renamed "inauthentic." That covers mass-produced content, including "AI-generated content made with generic or unoriginal templates." The same page says the reused-content rule did not change ([same page](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392)).

**Shorts revenue.** Views of "unedited clips from others' movies or TV shows" are left out of the revenue pool. Also, "Shorts over one minute in length that contain claimed content are blocked" ([Shorts monetization](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220)). That explains the 2 Shorts that were blocked worldwide.

**Creative Commons.** CC BY may only be used for content that is "licenseable under the CC BY license." It can't be added to a video that has a Content ID claim. Reusers must credit the title, author, source URL and licence ([YouTube CC help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2797468)). A CC tag deals with copyright only. Nothing in the policies says it protects reach. The TV-drama claim shows that some CC tags are applied by uploaders who don't own the content (our own experience, not a published statistic).

## 2. Desi comedians who allow or pay for clipping

- **No Pakistani or Indian comedian, comedy podcast or show found with a public paid clipping programme or a written "feel free to clip" note.** I searched Whop/Content Rewards, Vyro and the Indian clipping platforms. An Oct 2026 comparison of 8 Indian clipping platforms names no comedy creators or campaigns ([Musubiclip](https://musubiclip.com/blog/best-clipping-platforms-india/)). Treat this as unknown, not as proof that none exist. Campaign listings are only fully visible after signing in.
- **No well-known desi stand-up found who marks uploads CC.** The evidence points the other way. Samay Raina said "people re-uploading it is a problem I face" ([Legal500](https://www.legal500.com/intelligence/india/intellectual-property/re-uploaded-for-free%3B-samay-raina%27s-fight-against-copyright-infringement)). Kunal Kamra accused T-Series of a copyright strike on his stand-up video ([Asianet](https://newsable.asianetnews.com/india/-stop-being-a-stooge-kunal-kamra-slams-t-series-claims-music-label-s-copyright-strike-on-his-stand-up-video-shk-strjee)).
- **The payment setup exists in Pakistan.** Whop pays out to Pakistani bank accounts in PKR ([TechJuice](https://www.techjuice.pk/?p=225623)). Content Rewards has at least one campaign "only for clippers based in Pakistan." It is a finance brand, not comedy: $3 per 1K views, max $100 per clip ([listing](https://contentrewards.com/discover/baa092b2-25bc-4883-82eb-7c55cf9e8dc8)). Clipping typically pays $1–5 per 1K views, and Whop takes 10% ([Digiday](https://digiday.com/marketing/wtf-is-clipping/)). Vyro (MrBeast's platform) runs English-language campaigns at about $3 per 1K. That figure comes from a vendor blog ([OpusClip](https://www.opus.pro/blog/mrbeasts-vyro)).
- **YouTube's own Remix** credits the source "with a link to the video." For music videos, Cut is limited to 1–5 seconds, and creators can opt out ([YouTube Remix help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10623810)).

## 3. What working clip pages do

No rigorous study of desi comedy clip pages was found. The best available evidence:

- **Length.** Across 108,138 Shorts, median views peaked at 11–20 s (901), then fell to 188 at 46–60 s. The data has no genre split ([quso.ai](https://quso.ai/research/youtube-shorts-length)). A Vidooly study instead found 40–60 s best by average views ([Argil](https://www.argil.ai/blog/how-long-can-youtube-shorts-be)), so test this yourself.
- **First seconds.** The key Shorts metric is the share of times viewers "watched instead of swiping away." YouTube publishes no threshold for it. Public view counts have included replays and starts with no minimum watch time since March 2025, so use engaged views to compare Shorts ([Bytecap](https://www.bytecap.io/research/youtube-shorts-retention-benchmarks)).
- **Which moments.** Professional clip networks pick reactions, confrontations and awkward moments that work without context ([Streams Charts](https://streamscharts.com/news/manufactured-virality-how-paid-clipping-networks-became-streamer-marketings-hidden-engine)). Crowd-work clips follow a template: fast word-by-word subtitles and a one-idea caption such as "Heckler can't handle it." One comic posted a crowd-work clip every day for three months ([Guardian via Yahoo](https://uk.style.yahoo.com/viral-crowd-clips-remaking-standup-080039691.html)). In India, Reels clips under 30 s made comics famous ([HT Brunch](https://magzter.com/stories/lifestyle/Brunch/LAUGH-TRACK-WHATS-FUNNY-NOW)).
- **Luck plays a part.** Matt Rife's crowd-work clips sold 600,000 tickets in two days ([Pollstar](https://news.pollstar.com/2023/11/17/how-tiktok-comedy-hottie-matt-rife-sold-600000-tickets-across-256-shows-in-two-days/)), but he says he had no strategy ([Today](https://www.today.com/popculture/matt-rife-natural-selection-netflix-interview-rcna124392)).
- **Credit.** Under CC BY: title, author, source URL and licence, on screen or in the voice-over, plus the description ([YouTube CC help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2797468)).
- **How often to post.** No reliable data. Figures of 1–8 clips a day come from tool vendors and are opinion.

## 4. Desi comedy Shorts formats that are winning (2025–26)

- **Family / silent skits.** KL BRO Biju Rithvik's silent family skits reached about 78.6M subscribers in 2025, making him India's most-followed individual YouTuber ([Zee News](https://zeenews.india.com/photos/technology/india-s-top-youtube-creators-in-2025-from-gaming-comedy-to-vlogging-their-subscribers-may-blow-your-mind-2991226.html)). vidIQ estimates about 90M in Oct 2026 ([vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/UChxWiwCjtVeFkpfuLrnUPUQ)).
- **AI-made videos.** India's Bandar Apna Dost: 2.07B views, about $4.25M a year (estimate). Pakistan had 20 AI channels in its top-100 trending list, more than any other country, with 5.34B views (Oct 2025 data, [Kapwing](https://www.kapwing.com/blog/ai-slop-report-the-global-rise-of-low-quality-ai-videos/), [Net Influencer](https://www.netinfluencer.com/ai-generated-content-accounts-for-third-of-youtube-shorts-feed-kapwing-research-shows/)). Risk: 16 of the top 100 AI channels were later deleted or emptied ([NeoMam](https://neomam.com/projects/youtube-ai-slop-report/)).
- **Pakistani character skits.** Abdullah Tariq Khan's recurring characters Shanu and Aali rank #4 among Pakistan's top YouTubers ([Favikon, May 2026](https://www.favikon.com/blog/top-youtubers-pakistan)). Talha Ahmed, 16, from Karachi films phone-shot skits on Ramzan, Eid and tailors. He has 324K Instagram followers, and one video has 20M+ views ([Arab News](https://arabnews.pk/node/2597394/pakistan)).
- **Husband-wife.** The "Nadia… jee" couple reels went viral on a repeated one-line formula ([Buzzincontent](https://www.buzzincontent.com/news/meet-nadia-jee-the-pakistani-viral-sensation-taking-over-your-reels-feed-10618940)). No view counts were given.
- **Comedian-owned show formats and roasts (India).** Talent-hunt, blind-date and lie-detector shows (Madhur Model, Andha Pyaar, Lie Hard) get millions of views. Mint says the trend followed India's Got Latent and Pretty Good Roast Show ([Mint via Magzter](https://www.magzter.com/stories/newspaper/Mint-Bangalore/STANDUP-COMICS-TURNING-TO-SIGNATURE-FORMATS-FOR-REACH)).
- **Crowd work and podcasts (Pakistan).** Pakistani crowd-work clips exist, e.g. [Obaid, Oct 2025](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lsx33EfilNo) and [Abbas Bukhari](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NN2Y-Cr8IS8). View counts are unknown. Podcasts are listed among Pakistan's main YouTube formats ([Dunya News](https://dunyanews.tv/en/Technology/924121-how-2025-became-a-record-year-for-pakistani-youtubers)). Nadir Ali's channel has 1.79B views in total ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nadir_Ali_(comedian))).
- **2D cartoons:** no desi-specific data found (unknown).

## What this means for Punchline Bazaar

1. **The format itself is the likely cause of the 1-view median, not bad clip choice.** A channel made only of other people's clips fits YouTube's description of an "aggregating" channel. That label applies to the whole channel, and a CC licence does not lift it. If most of the 94 Shorts went up after about 1 Oct 2026, this change alone could explain the 10x gap. This is an inference: YouTube gives no indicator.
2. **The trailing AI comment almost certainly doesn't count as added value.** It is template-based and comes after many viewers have already swiped. Either make the commentary the main part of each Short, from the first second, or stop posting plain clips.
3. **Best option: turn the channel into an original format.** Recurring Urdu characters, family or husband-wife skits, or a recurring AI host who roasts or ranks moments, with any clip used as short evidence. These are the formats winning in Pakistan, and the owner's original channels already get 10x the views. YouTube says reach gets re-evaluated when a channel shifts to original work.
4. **If clips stay, get written permission.** Message 10–20 small Pakistani comics and open-mic acts directly; none publish a clipping offer, so ask. Offer on-screen credit and a link. Also check Content Rewards for Pakistan-only campaigns. Even then, add real commentary, because YouTube hasn't said whether permitted clips avoid the reach cut.
5. **Packaging to test:** 11–30 s; open on the reaction or the setup line; word-by-word Urdu or Roman-Urdu captions; one-idea titles; never over 60 s; CC credit in the format shown above.
6. **Judge results in Studio, not by public view counts.** Track "viewed vs swiped away" and engaged views per Short. Change one variable a week, and avoid identical templates (the July 2025 "inauthentic" rule).
