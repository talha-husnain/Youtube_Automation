# Background music

Put royalty-free tracks here (mp3, m4a, wav, ogg, aac or flac). Cricket shorts
with "background music under the voice" ticked use a random one, quietly under
the voice.

Free sources that allow use in YouTube videos:
- YouTube Audio Library: studio.youtube.com, Audio library. Pick tracks marked
  "no attribution required".
- Pixabay Music: pixabay.com/music.

Never put a film song or a label's track here: it would be claimed.
